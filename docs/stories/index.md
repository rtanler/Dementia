- **[Four O'Clock: A Family's Journey Through Sundowning](four-oclock/index.md)**

    ![Four O'Clock](./four-oclock/cover.png)
    When a gentle grandmother becomes a different person every afternoon at 4 PM, one family learns the name *sundowning* and builds a new ritual of light, tea, and presence to meet the hour with a plan instead of fear. A story about recognizing patterns, calling the helpline, and the hands that remember what the mind forgets.
