# List of MicroSims for Theory of Knowledge

Interactive Micro Simulations to help students learn Theory of Knowledge fundamentals.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **[Behavior Management Decision Tree](./behavior-management-decision-tree/index.md)**

    ![Behavior Management Decision Tree](./behavior-management-decision-tree/behavior-management-decision-tree.png)

    Apply systematic problem-solving to challenging behaviors in moderate-stage dementia (Bloom Level 3 - Apply)

-   **[Beta-Amyloid Production and Aggregation](./beta-amyloid-production-and-aggregation/index.md)**

    ![Beta-Amyloid Production and Aggregation](./beta-amyloid-production-and-aggregation/beta-amyloid-production-and-aggregation.png)

    Interactive diagram tracing the seven stages of beta-amyloid production, from APP protein cleavage to mature amyloid plaques, with explanations of how each stage relates to Alzheimer's disease.

-   **[Brain Regions Affected in Rare Tauopathies](./brain-regions-affected-in-rare-tauopathies/index.md)**

    ![Brain Regions Affected in Rare Tauopathies](./brain-regions-affected-in-rare-tauopathies/brain-regions-affected-in-rare-tauopathies.png)

    Interactive diagram comparing the distinct patterns of brain damage in Pick's disease, corticobasal degeneration, and progressive supranuclear palsy, and how each pattern produces characteristic symptoms.

-   **[Building Cognitive Reserve Over a Lifetime](./building-cognitive-reserve-over-a-lifetime/index.md)**

    ![Building Cognitive Reserve Over a Lifetime](./building-cognitive-reserve-over-a-lifetime/building-cognitive-reserve-over-a-lifetime.png)

    Understand how cognitive reserve accumulates through life experiences and activities (Bloom Level 2 - Understand)

-   **[Dementia Stages Interactive Timeline](./dementia-stages-interactive-timeline/index.md)**

    ![Dementia Stages Interactive Timeline](./dementia-stages-interactive-timeline/dementia-stages-interactive-timeline.png)

    Analyze how dementia progresses through stages over time and how different domains decline at different rates (Bloom Level 4 - Analyze)

-   **[Dementia Types Comparison Matrix](./dementia-types-comparison-matrix/index.md)**

    ![Dementia Types Comparison Matrix](./dementia-types-comparison-matrix/dementia-types-comparison-matrix.png)

    Analyze and compare the distinguishing features of major dementia types (Bloom Level 4 - Analyze)

-   **[Dementia Types Prevalence Chart](./dementia-types-prevalence-chart/index.md)**

    ![Dementia Types Prevalence Chart](./dementia-types-prevalence-chart/dementia-types-prevalence-chart.png)

    Understand the relative prevalence of different dementia types (Bloom Level 2 - Understand)

-   **[Early-Stage Dementia Characteristics](./early-stage-characteristics/index.md)**

    ![Early-Stage Dementia Characteristics](./early-stage-characteristics/early-stage-characteristics.png)

    Interactive wheel diagram showing characteristics of early-stage dementia across five key domains - cognitive, functional, personality, behavioral, and mood - highlighting what is impaired versus preserved.

-   **[Early-Stage Dementia Characteristics](./early-stage-dementia-characteristics/index.md)**

    ![Early-Stage Dementia Characteristics](./early-stage-dementia-characteristics/early-stage-dementia-characteristics.png)

    Understand the characteristics of early-stage dementia across cognitive, functional, personality, behavioral, and mood domains (Bloom Level 2 - Understand)

    ![Executive Function in Daily Life](./executive-function-in-daily-life/executive-function-in-daily-life.png)

    Understand how executive function coordinates multiple cognitive processes to complete everyday tasks (Bloom Level 2 - Understand)

    ![graph-viewer](./graph-viewer/learning-graph.png)

    Interactive MicroSim for graph-viewer.

    ![Healthy Tau vs. Abnormal Tau](./healthy-tau-vs-abnormal-tau/healthy-tau-vs-abnormal-tau.png)

    Interactive side-by-side comparison of normal tau protein stabilizing microtubules in a healthy neuron versus hyperphosphorylated tau forming neurofibrillary tangles in Alzheimer's disease.

-   **[How Memory Formation Works](./how-memory-formation-works/index.md)**

    ![How Memory Formation Works](./how-memory-formation-works/how-memory-formation-works.png)

-   **[Neuroplasticity in Action](./neuroplasticity-in-action/index.md)**

    ![Neuroplasticity in Action](./neuroplasticity-in-action/neuroplasticity-in-action.png)

-   **[Protein Aggregation Comparison](./protein-aggregation-comparison/index.md)**

    ![Protein Aggregation Comparison](./protein-aggregation-comparison/protein-aggregation-comparison.png)

    ![Synaptic Communication](./synaptic-communication/synaptic-communication.png)

    Interactive diagram showing how neurons communicate across a synapse using electrical and chemical signals, with notes on how dementia damages each step of the process.

    ![The Brain's Major Regions](./the-brain-s-major-regions/the-brain-s-major-regions.png)

    Interactive diagram to identify the major regions of the brain and their basic functions, including the cerebral cortex, four lobes, hippocampus, and cerebellum.
