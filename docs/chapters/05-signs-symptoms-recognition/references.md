# References: Signs, Symptoms, and Early Recognition

A curated list of high-quality resources to deepen your understanding of early signs and symptoms of dementia. Wikipedia articles offer free, well-sourced overviews; textbooks provide authoritative depth; online resources from major dementia organizations give practical, up-to-date guidance.

1. [Mild cognitive impairment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mild_cognitive_impairment) - Wikipedia - Covers the cognitive state between normal aging and dementia, including criteria, causes, and progression risk. Essential background for understanding early recognition.

2. [Memory and aging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memory_and_aging) - Wikipedia - Explains how memory changes with normal aging and how these changes differ from early dementia, helping readers calibrate their concerns about symptoms.

3. [Dementia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dementia) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive article with a detailed symptoms section that catalogs cognitive, behavioral, and functional changes appearing in early stages.

4. The 36-Hour Day: A Family Guide to Caring for People Who Have Alzheimer Disease, Other Dementias, and Memory Loss (6th ed.) - Nancy L. Mace and Peter V. Rabins - Johns Hopkins University Press - Includes practical chapters describing the subtle early signs families notice first, making it an excellent companion to this chapter on recognition.

5. The Alzheimer's Action Plan - P. Murali Doraiswamy and Lisa P. Gwyther - St. Martin's Griffin - Written for families facing possible dementia, this book walks readers through recognizing symptoms and taking the right first steps, mirroring this chapter's goals.

6. [10 Early Signs and Symptoms of Alzheimer's](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/10_signs) - Alzheimer's Association - The widely cited checklist of early warning signs, distinguishing dementia from normal aging. A practical, patient-friendly companion to this chapter.

7. [Memory, Forgetfulness, and Aging: What's Normal and What's Not?](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/memory-loss-and-forgetfulness/memory-forgetfulness-and-aging-whats-normal-and-whats-not) - National Institute on Aging - Federal guidance on telling age-related memory changes from signs of dementia, directly relevant to this chapter's focus.

8. [Symptoms of dementia](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/symptoms/) - NHS (UK) - Concise, accessible list of common early and later symptoms with links to related topics. Useful for readers wanting a quick, reliable symptom guide.

9. [Early signs of dementia](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/symptoms-and-diagnosis/symptoms) - Alzheimer's Society (UK) - Patient-focused descriptions of early cognitive, emotional, and practical symptoms, expanding on the recognition themes introduced in this chapter.

10. [Dementia](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/dementia/symptoms-causes/syc-20352013) - Mayo Clinic - Trusted medical overview with clear symptom categories and guidance on when to seek medical advice, reinforcing the chapter's early-recognition message.
