# References: Managing Challenging Behaviors

A curated list of high-quality resources to deepen your understanding of how to manage challenging behaviors in dementia. Wikipedia articles offer free, well-sourced overviews; textbooks provide authoritative depth; online resources from major dementia organizations give practical, up-to-date guidance.

1. [Sundowning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundowning) - Wikipedia - Describes the late-day confusion, agitation, and restlessness common in dementia, including causes and management strategies discussed in this chapter.

2. [Behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavioral_and_psychological_symptoms_of_dementia) - Wikipedia - Overview of the cluster of non-cognitive symptoms such as agitation, aggression, and hallucinations that families and clinicians must manage.

3. [Wandering (dementia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wandering_(dementia)) - Wikipedia - Focused article on wandering behavior, its causes, risks, and prevention strategies, a core topic in this chapter.

4. Learning to Speak Alzheimer's - Joanne Koenig Coste - Mariner Books - Describes the "habilitation" approach, which reframes difficult behaviors as communication and offers practical responses that align with this chapter's strategies.

5. Creating Moments of Joy Along the Alzheimer's Journey - Jolene Brackey - Purdue University Press - Offers caregiver-tested ideas for redirecting difficult behaviors and creating positive interactions, a strong practical complement to this chapter.

6. [Managing Personality and Behavior Changes in Alzheimer's](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/managing-personality-and-behavior-changes) - National Institute on Aging - Federal guide to understanding and responding to behavior changes. Directly supports the chapter's practical management focus.

7. [Behaviors](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/behaviors) - Alzheimer's Association - Comprehensive caregiver guidance on specific behaviors including aggression, anxiety, sundowning, and wandering, mirroring this chapter's topics.

8. [Changes in behaviour](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/symptoms-and-diagnosis/how-dementia-progresses/changes-behaviour) - Alzheimer's Society (UK) - Accessible explanations of why behaviors change and how caregivers can respond constructively, reinforcing this chapter's message.

9. [Caregiver's Guide to Understanding Dementia Behaviors](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) - Family Caregiver Alliance - Detailed caregiver-oriented guide to behaviors such as agitation, hallucinations, and wandering, with concrete response techniques.

10. [Alzheimer's: Managing sleep problems](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/alzheimers-disease/in-depth/alzheimers/art-20047832) - Mayo Clinic - Practical medical guidance on sleep disturbance and sundowning, a common and difficult behavior cluster addressed in this chapter.
