# References: Brain Anatomy and Function

A curated list of high-quality resources to deepen your understanding of brain anatomy and function as it relates to dementia. Wikipedia articles offer free, well-sourced overviews; textbooks provide authoritative depth; online resources from major dementia organizations give practical, up-to-date guidance.

1. [Human brain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_brain) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive overview of the human brain, its structure, regions, and functions. A foundational reference for understanding the anatomy that dementia affects.

2. [Hippocampus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hippocampus) - Wikipedia - Detailed article on the hippocampus, the memory-forming structure that is one of the earliest and most severely affected areas in Alzheimer's disease.

3. [Cerebral cortex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cerebral_cortex) - Wikipedia - Covers the cortex, its lobes, and its roles in thinking, language, and behavior. Important for understanding how dementia disrupts higher cognitive functions.

4. Principles of Neural Science (6th ed.) - Eric R. Kandel, John D. Koester, Sarah H. Mack, and Steven A. Siegelbaum - McGraw-Hill - The authoritative neuroscience textbook used worldwide, providing in-depth but well-written coverage of brain structure and function relevant to this chapter.

5. Neuroanatomy: An Atlas of Structures, Sections, and Systems - Duane E. Haines - Wolters Kluwer - A widely used neuroanatomy reference with clear illustrations of brain regions affected by dementia, ideal for readers who want visual depth beyond the chapter.

6. [How the Brain Works](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/brain-health/how-brain-works) - National Institute on Aging - Plain-language overview of brain regions, neurons, and how aging and disease affect them. Complements the chapter with authoritative, accessible explanations.

7. [Brain Tour](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/what-is-alzheimers/brain_tour) - Alzheimer's Association - Interactive, patient-friendly walkthrough of brain structures and how Alzheimer's damages them. Reinforces the anatomical concepts introduced in this chapter.

8. [Brain Basics: Know Your Brain](https://www.ninds.nih.gov/health-information/public-education/brain-basics/brain-basics-know-your-brain) - National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke - Federal introduction to brain anatomy and function written for general audiences, offering a trusted companion to this chapter's material.

9. [How the brain works](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/symptoms-and-diagnosis/how-dementia-progresses/how-brain-affected) - Alzheimer's Society (UK) - Clear explanation of how different brain areas support cognition and how dementia affects them, bridging anatomy and clinical symptoms.

10. [Brain](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/22638-brain) - Cleveland Clinic - Accessible medical overview of brain anatomy, regions, and common disorders, giving readers a reliable reference for the structures discussed in this chapter.
