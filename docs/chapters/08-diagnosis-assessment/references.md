# References: Diagnosis and Assessment

A curated list of high-quality resources to deepen your understanding of how dementia is diagnosed and assessed. Wikipedia articles offer free, well-sourced overviews; textbooks provide authoritative depth; online resources from major dementia organizations give practical, up-to-date guidance.

1. [Mini–Mental State Examination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mini%E2%80%93Mental_State_Examination) - Wikipedia - Describes one of the most widely used cognitive screening tools in dementia assessment, including its structure, scoring, and limitations.

2. [Montreal Cognitive Assessment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal_Cognitive_Assessment) - Wikipedia - Overview of the MoCA, a widely used test that can detect milder cognitive changes than the MMSE, directly relevant to early diagnosis.

3. [Neuropsychological test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuropsychological_test) - Wikipedia - Explains the broader field of cognitive testing used in dementia workups, including test types and what each assesses.

4. Practical Dementia Care - Peter V. Rabins, Constantine G. Lyketsos, and Cynthia D. Steele - Oxford University Press - Provides a clinically grounded framework for the diagnostic workup of dementia, complementing this chapter's overview of the assessment process.

5. The Alzheimer's Action Plan - P. Murali Doraiswamy and Lisa P. Gwyther - St. Martin's Griffin - Walks readers through what to expect at each step of a dementia evaluation, from primary care through specialist visits, matching this chapter's practical orientation.

6. [How Is Alzheimer's Disease Diagnosed?](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-symptoms-and-diagnosis/how-alzheimers-disease-diagnosed) - National Institute on Aging - Plain-language federal guide to the diagnostic process, including medical history, cognitive tests, and imaging. Authoritative and directly relevant.

7. [Diagnosis](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis) - Alzheimer's Association - Patient-friendly overview of the dementia diagnostic process, tests used, and how to prepare for an evaluation. Reinforces this chapter's content.

8. [Diagnosis of dementia](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/diagnosis/) - NHS (UK) - Accessible guide to tests and assessments used when dementia is suspected, useful for readers wanting a concise step-by-step overview.

9. [Diagnosing dementia](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/symptoms-and-diagnosis/diagnosis) - Alzheimer's Society (UK) - Detailed pages on memory assessment services, brain scans, and follow-up, closely matching this chapter's assessment topics.

10. [Dementia: Diagnosis & treatment](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/dementia/diagnosis-treatment/drc-20352019) - Mayo Clinic - Trusted clinical overview of how dementia is diagnosed, with descriptions of cognitive tests, labs, and brain imaging used in workups.
